How to read this

  • Identity and quantity are recapitulated. Which residue is substituted, where, and at what rate — the paper's actual claims — match Slava's reference across proteins, cell lines and tissue.
  • The two ML-feature columns are the only real divergence, and they diverge by design: SpectralSim/RTScore are MSBooster neural-net predictions, so a newer MSBooster gives correlated-but-shifted values. This is the "engine upgrade" surfacing exactly where it can — in learned features, never in identities or ratios.
  • Scope. Compared on the Step 7 substitution table, the level at which a Slava reference exists. 10 human tRNA samples; the E. coli panel was still running at report time.
